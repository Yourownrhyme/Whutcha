Terms of Service

1. Acceptance of Terms & Eligibility

By registering for or accessing Whutcha ("the Platform"), you agree to comply with and be bound by these Terms of Service. You must be at least 18 years of age or the age of legal majority in your jurisdiction to operate a broadcaster account.

2. 0% Platform Fee Model & Direct Settlement

Whutcha operates strictly as a zero-commission technology protocol for video donations. The Platform charges 0% fees on viewer donations. All payments flow directly between the viewer and the broadcaster via connected payment gateways. Whutcha never escrows fiat funds on its own balance sheet without explicit instruction.

3. Broadcast Rights, Content Licensing & Public Display

By submitting a video-donation or requesting a live Co-Stream, the viewer grants the recipient broadcaster a non-exclusive, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free license to stream, record, edit, and publicly display the submitted media during live broadcasts and subsequent derivative video on demand (VODs, YouTube clips, TikTok/Instagram Shorts).

4. Zero-TOS Content Moderation & Streamer Indemnity

Broadcasters retain sole editorial control over all media sent to their broadcast. Whutcha provides a dedicated pre-moderation queue ("Green Room") allowing broadcasters to preview video donations before sending them to the OBS overlay. Broadcasters acknowledge that unmoderated live Co-Streams are admitted at their own discretion. Hate speech, illegal conduct, explicit violence, non-consensual content, and copyright infringement are strictly prohibited.

5. Chargeback Shield & Anti-Fraud Escrow

Viewers agree that digital video roasts and live broadcast calls are fulfilled immediately upon approval and broadcast. Fraudulent disputes, abusive chargebacks, or automated payment disputes will result in permanent hardware-level IP blacklisting from the Whutcha network and forfeiture of pending claims.

6. Prohibited Acts & Immediate Hardware Bans

Users shall not upload malicious code, attempt denial-of-service attacks against the WebRTC bridge, forge donation payloads, or impersonate other creators. Violations trigger automated telemetry detection resulting in immediate account termination without prior notice.

7. Service Availability & Disclaimers

Whutcha is provided "as is" and "as available". While we maintain high-availability architecture with low-latency WebRTC routing, we do not warrant uninterrupted broadcast connectivity or third-party streaming platform compliance.

Privacy Policy

1. Data Minimization Principles

Whutcha adheres to strict privacy-by-design. We collect only the minimum technical metadata necessary to authenticate broadcasters, verify WebRTC signaling channels, and deliver video donations to OBS browser sources.

2. Information We Collect

We process: (a) Broadcaster account credentials (email and OAuth identifiers); (b) Ephemeral video blobs uploaded by fans for live donation playback; (c) Connection telemetry (browser agent, WebRTC candidate latency) to diagnose streaming issues.

3. Ephemeral Video Storage & Deletion

Fan-uploaded video messages are stored temporarily in secured cloud buckets with private signed URLs. Rejected donations are permanently deleted. Played video donations are preserved only if explicitly enabled by the creator for highlight clip export.

4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

In accordance with GDPR (Articles 15-22) and CCPA, you have the right to access, rectify, or request complete erasure of your broadcaster profile and associated media at any time. Broadcasters can trigger a 1-click channel delist and data wipe directly inside their Settings menu.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. Local storage is utilized solely to maintain broadcaster session tokens, store OBS overlay keys, and save user layout preferences.

6. Security & Encryption

All communication is secured via TLS 1.3 encryption. WebRTC media streams are encrypted point-to-point via SRTP/DTLS. Database access is strictly guarded with Row-Level Security (RLS) policies preventing unauthorized data access.
