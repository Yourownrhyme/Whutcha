Privacy Policy

1. Data Minimization Principles

Whutcha adheres to strict privacy-by-design. We collect only the minimum technical metadata necessary to authenticate broadcasters, coordinate low-latency WebRTC streams, and deliver video donations to OBS browser sources.

2. Information We Collect

We process: (a) Broadcaster account credentials (email and OAuth identifiers); (b) Ephemeral video blobs uploaded by fans for live donation playback; (c) Connection telemetry (browser agent, WebRTC candidate latency) to diagnose streaming issues.

3. Ephemeral Video Storage & Deletion

Fan-uploaded video messages are stored temporarily in secured cloud buckets with private signed URLs. Rejected donations are permanently deleted. Played video donations are preserved only if explicitly enabled by the creator for highlight clip export.

4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

In accordance with GDPR (Articles 15-22) and CCPA, you have the right to access, rectify, or request complete erasure of your broadcaster profile and associated media at any time. Broadcasters can trigger a 1-click channel delist and data wipe directly inside their Settings menu.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. Local storage is utilized solely to maintain broadcaster session tokens, store OBS overlay keys, and save user layout preferences.

6. Security & Encryption

All communication is secured via TLS 1.3 encryption. WebRTC media streams are encrypted point-to-point via SRTP/DTLS. Database access is strictly guarded with Row-Level Security (RLS) policies preventing unauthorized data access.
