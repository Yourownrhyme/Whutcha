Terms of Service

1. Acceptance of Terms & Eligibility

By registering for or accessing Whutcha ("the Platform"), you agree to comply with and be bound by these Terms of Service. You must be at least 18 years of age or the age of legal majority in your jurisdiction to operate a broadcaster account.

2. 0% Platform Fee Model & Direct Settlement

Whutcha operates as a zero-commission platform for video donations. The Platform charges 0% fees on viewer donations. All payments flow directly between the viewer and the broadcaster via connected payment gateways.

3. Broadcast Rights, Content Licensing & Public Display

By submitting a video-donation or requesting a live Co-Stream, the viewer grants the recipient broadcaster a non-exclusive, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free license to stream, record, edit, and publicly display the submitted media during live broadcasts and subsequent derivative video on demand (VODs, YouTube clips, TikTok/Instagram Shorts).

4. Pre-Moderation & Streamer Controls

Broadcasters retain sole editorial control over all media sent to their broadcast. Whutcha provides a dedicated pre-moderation queue ("Green Room") allowing broadcasters to preview video donations before sending them to the OBS overlay. Broadcasters acknowledge that live Co-Streams are admitted at their own discretion. Hate speech, illegal conduct, explicit violence, non-consensual content, and copyright infringement are strictly prohibited.

5. Refund Policy & Anti-Fraud Protection

Viewers agree that digital video messages and live broadcast calls are fulfilled immediately upon approval and broadcast. Fraudulent disputes or automated payment abuse will result in permanent blocking from the platform.

6. Acceptable Use

Users shall not upload malicious files, attempt denial-of-service attacks, forge donation payloads, or impersonate other creators. Violations result in immediate account suspension.

7. Service Availability & Disclaimers

Whutcha is provided "as is" and "as available". While we maintain high-availability architecture with low-latency WebRTC routing, we do not warrant uninterrupted broadcast connectivity or third-party streaming platform compliance.

Privacy Policy

1. Data Minimization Principles

Whutcha adheres to strict privacy-by-design. We collect only the minimum technical metadata necessary to authenticate broadcasters, verify WebRTC signaling channels, and deliver video donations to OBS browser sources.

2. Information We Collect

We process: (a) Broadcaster account credentials (email and OAuth identifiers from Twitch, Discord, or Google); (b) Ephemeral video blobs uploaded by fans for live donation playback; (c) Connection telemetry (browser agent, WebRTC candidate latency) to diagnose streaming issues.

3. Ephemeral Video Storage & Deletion

Fan-uploaded video messages are stored temporarily in secured cloud buckets with private signed URLs. Rejected donations are permanently deleted. Played video donations are preserved only if explicitly enabled by the creator for highlight clip export.

4. Data Privacy & Account Deletion

You have the right to access, update, or request complete deletion of your broadcaster profile and associated media at any time directly inside your Settings menu.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. Local storage is utilized solely to maintain broadcaster session tokens, store OBS overlay keys, and save user layout preferences.

6. Security & Encryption

All communication is secured via TLS 1.3 encryption. WebRTC media streams are encrypted point-to-point via SRTP/DTLS. Database access is strictly guarded with Row-Level Security (RLS) policies preventing unauthorized data access.
